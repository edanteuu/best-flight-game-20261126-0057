**Nusa Airlift** — a chase-view prop-plane courier game over a volcanic island archipelago. Fly a little red-and-white airlift plane through glowing target rings, mind your fuel and your airspeed, and put her down on runway 36 on Nusa Besar. Three missions with different goals: **Ring Run** (thread eight rings, then land), **Island Hop** (five far-flung rings on a three-quarter tank — throttle discipline decides whether you swim), and **Storm Landing** (three rings in gusting wind, then down before the squall closes in). Pure Canvas 2D with a hand-built pseudo-3D projection, original vector art, a lit glass HUD (altitude, airspeed, compass tape, artificial horizon, throttle and fuel gauges, ring counter, timer and score), and sounds synthesised live with WebAudio — no libraries, no network requests, no copied art or music. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best flight game" bounty, $500 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD — pitch and bank · W / S (or Shift / Ctrl) — throttle · Space — flaps (slows you for landing) · mouse drag steers too · click the mission buttons · P pause · R restart · M sound on/off · 1 / 2 / 3 pick a mission.

Screenshots in `screenshots/` are real renders of the game itself, taken with `?shot=start|play|rings|land&seed=1`: the title over the archipelago, a banked ring run past the volcano, a low ring over the sea, and the final approach to runway 36.
